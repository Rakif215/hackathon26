The problem

And too much to check.

Read the patient’s whole history

Check her pills match the prescription

MedGuard listens to the visit. Everything it hears is checked against her data in Exasol: the record, blood tests, pill photos and the official leaflets. It suggests a question or a check and shows where it comes from. When it isn’t sure, it asks. The doctor decides. Then: “Let me show you.” Switch to the live demo.
